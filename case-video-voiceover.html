Case study

Automated Inventory Video Script & Voiceover Workflow

An automation workflow that helped non-technical staff turn existing vehicle-page details into multiple expert-style video scripts and voiceover assets.

Tools used

Make.com Claude API ElevenLabs Email automation Webhooks Prompt design
Vehicle details email
Webhook trigger
3 Claude styles
ElevenLabs voices
Email response

Context

Dealers often create videos for every unit in inventory, especially pre-owned vehicles where condition, color, options, trim, and small details can make each car feel distinct. Customers want enough information to understand and visualize the vehicle before deciding whether to come in for a test drive.

The broader goal was to simplify the car-shopping journey by giving users more of the information they need up front. The challenge was that good inventory videos depended on a vehicle expert who knew the cars, understood the features, and knew what was worth saying.

Problem

When that expert was not available, the process still needed to continue. Much of the raw information already existed on vehicle pages, but someone had to read it, interpret it, and turn it into a useful script. The workflow needed to make that possible for staff who could gather the source details, even if they were not product experts themselves.

Constraints

  • Needed a simple trigger
  • Needed to work from existing vehicle-page descriptions or details pasted into an email body
  • Needed outputs returned in a usable format
  • Needed to control token usage and prompt quality
  • Needed to remain understandable for non-technical users
  • Needed to help staff who knew where to find the vehicle information, even if they were not product experts

Workflow architecture

A near-real-time Make workflow turns vehicle-page information into three script styles and matching voiceover files, then emails the assets back to the sender.

Vehicle page details

The team pastes existing vehicle information from the website into the email body.

Webhook trigger

The email starts the Make workflow, so staff do not need to open or understand the automation itself.

Claude branches

The workflow routes into three script styles, each with branded tone, video structure, walkaround guidance, length, and format requirements.

ElevenLabs voices

Each script branch is converted into a voiceover file using ElevenLabs.

Email response

The sender receives the scripts and MP3 files back by email in a near-real-time loop, typically under a minute.

My role

Designed the Make automation flow, structured prompts for different video styles, connected the Claude script-generation branches, integrated ElevenLabs voice synthesis on each branch, and tested whether the outputs captured the kind of feature-aware scripting an expert would normally provide.

Outcome / value

  • Reduced manual scripting effort
  • Created repeatable video content workflows for multiple script styles
  • Improved consistency across inventory scripts
  • Supported 250+ vehicle videos generated over time
  • Returned usable scripts and MP3 files in roughly 30 seconds to under a minute
  • Made the workflow usable by staff who could gather source details without needing deep product expertise
  • Demonstrated how AI could support marketing content production without replacing human review

What I learned

The three video styles came from real staff feedback: what they noticed, what they wanted more control over, and what made the outputs more useful in practice. The workflow worked best as a loop between automation, human review, and real-world testing, with options staff could choose from and continue improving over time.
